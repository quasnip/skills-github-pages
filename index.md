# My GRC Journey
This is a GitHub Page dedicated to documenting my learning experiences as I go from student to certified.
## Why?
Auditing information systems requires heavy documentation and the ability to do deep research. By making a public record of my journey, I can look back on my progress and hopefully inspire to others who are in the same position. 
## How?
This page will develop alongside my level of experience and therefore will change as time goes on. My intention is to publish my deep dives, analyze current large-scale security events, or just share things I find interesting.

### Let's see where this goes!
